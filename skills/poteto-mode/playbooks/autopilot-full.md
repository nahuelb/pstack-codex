### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never the PRs. One owner runs each PR from build to merge, and nothing merges without your clean swarm verdict.** For "autopilot this queue", "full autopilot", and one-owner-per-PR programs. The job is a queue of independent PRs handed over to drive to merged with full autonomy. Orchestrate runs a standing program whose coordinator lands verified work itself and whose workers never merge. Here each PR's owner carries the whole lifecycle, while the main agent keeps verification, countersigns, and audits.

1. **Mark the operator's items and honor state-then-wait.** Items the operator names stay hers. She reviews and she clicks, and no owner merges one. When she asks for the protocol or the plan to be stated, deliver the statement and stop. Execution starts only on her explicit go. That go authorizes creating a goal with the full program objective and a thread heartbeat until the queue is done.
2. **Spawn one owner per PR with the full lifecycle and an early trail.** One isolated Codex worktree agent per PR owns build, the first push, a ready GitHub PR, self-proof, skeptical Bugbot triage, `$unslop`, `$no-comments`, a rebase onto current trunk, babysit to green, and the merge itself. Within about 15 minutes, every owner starts an uncommitted `decisions.tsv` trail, pushes its first branch snapshot, and opens the PR ready. Open the PR before self-proof so its URL, decisions, and checks form a durable trail. The rebase precedes babysit. Merge remains gated by step 4 and the user's explicit authority.
3. **Run owners in true parallel and never stack.** Many owners can run at once when PRs are self-contained. Give each one a branch, disjoint files, and an isolated worktree. Only overlapping work serializes. Self-contained PRs branch from main, and sequenced work uses merge-then-branch. An owner that must split a dependent change may hold a short private base-branch stack.
4. **Swarm-verify every merge-ready head before its merge.** At the owner's merge-ready head SHA, fan out parallel independent verifiers per the **swarm** skill and aggregate one verdict. Re-run the gates, prove the load-bearing behavior on the real surface, and audit the receipts and diff. One regression lane runs the same scenario on current trunk. If trunk lacks the feature, record that fact and gate the new behavior plus the end state the user expects. The live lane is the floor. No merge occurs without the main agent's clean verdict. Findings return to the owner for fix-forward. A new head gets a fresh swarm and verdict.
5. **On a clean verdict the owner merges and takes the next item.** The owner merges only from a head freshly rebased onto trunk. The merge-ready report pins that SHA. If trunk moves again, the patch-id rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` governs re-verification. The owner squash-merges its own PR through `gh` and picks up its next self-contained item. The user's full-autonomy grant plus the main agent's clean verdict authorizes that merge. Operator-named items stop at merge-ready and wait for her click.
6. **Run the main-agent layer.** A genuinely new raise of a pinned gate or budget value needs a fresh countersign after verifier proof. Use the authorized thread heartbeat for audit ticks around every 30 minutes. Never hold a shell open to sleep. At each tick, re-read this playbook from trunk and inspect the live goal. Audit the operation against both. Probe each owner through supported live status, collect decision trails, and count only side effects as progress. A lane that exceeds its expected runtime without a side effect is stuck. Stop it, reconcile partial state, and dispatch at most one bounded replacement. When merges batch, run a retro pass and a post-merge bot-comment sweep.
7. **Stand down instantly on the operator's stop.** Her hold or stand-down reaches every owner as a zero-writes order immediately. Owners hold their briefs until she releases them.

**Reply:** the queue with each PR's owner, state, and head SHA; each verdict and the swarm that produced it; what merged and what each owner took next; countersigns granted and why; open operator gates; where the collected decision trails live.
